# Frequently asked questions

## Where can I find a piece of equipment?

Open **EXPLORE** and follow the location tree to its Room and Rack. You can also use global Search if you know its name.

## Why can't I see an edit button?

Your account may have viewing access only. Some features are available only to users who organize infrastructure.

## How can I go back to rooms or racks?

Use the navigation tree or the location links displayed on screen.

## I placed a rack, but it disappeared when I returned. Why?

If you were editing the plan, check whether you selected **SAVE LAYOUT**. The change may not have been saved.

## What does CONNECTED mean?

It means Site Mapper has a connection recorded for that terminal. It does not prove electrical power is flowing right now.

## Why does my equipment appear but show no values?

Inventory information and readings are separate. Check the status and latest reading time.

## Can I view previous readings?

Yes, when stored readings are available. Open the history section and choose **24H** or **7D**.

## What if something seems wrong?

Check that you have opened the correct equipment. If the issue continues, share its name, location and status message with your administrator.
