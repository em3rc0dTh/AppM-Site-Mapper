# Understand how your infrastructure is organized

Think about finding equipment inside a building. First choose the location, then the room, then the rack and finally the equipment.

![How to find equipment in a room](/help/diagrams/en/places.svg)

## Main parts of your infrastructure

| On-screen name | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Network** | A group of sites |
| **Site** | A facility or location |
| **Structure** | A building or structure |
| **Level** | A floor or level |
| **Room** | A room |
| **Bay / Position** | An area and a place inside the room |
| **Rack** | The cabinet that holds equipment |
| **Device** | A main unit inside a rack |
| **Equipment** | A component inside that unit |

## A simple example

Suppose you need to check a breaker in a panel:

1. Open the right **Network**.
2. Choose the **Site** and building.
3. Open the **Level** and **Room**.
4. Choose the **Rack** containing the unit.
5. Open the **Device**, find its **Panel** and select the breaker.

You don't need to memorize every term. The navigation tree helps you move through the locations.

## Rack or panel?

A **rack** holds equipment. A **panel** is part of a piece of equipment and may contain several breaker positions.

**You're done when:** you can explain where a piece of equipment is located and how to reach it from EXPLORE.
