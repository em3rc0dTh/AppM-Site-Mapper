# Edit a site or building boundary

When the shape of a space changes, you can adjust its outline by moving points on the drawing.

![Where spaces belong](/help/diagrams/en/places.svg)

## Before you start

- Open the correct **Site** or **Structure** and check its name and location.
- You need editing access. If the option is missing, ask your Site Mapper administrator.
- Check what the space contains. Moving a boundary should not leave existing buildings or rooms outside their intended location.

## Change the outline

1. Find and open the right space from **EXPLORE**.
2. Select the option to **edit its boundary**.
3. On the drawing, select a point on the outline and drag it to its new position.
4. If you need another point, select a line segment. You can remove a point as long as **at least three points** remain.
5. Review the full shape: edges should not cross and the outline must be closed.
6. Confirm the change in the editor. Reopen the space to check the result.

## If the change is rejected

The edges may cross, the outline may be invalid, or somebody may have changed the space while you were editing. Review the current drawing before trying again.

## Remember

**Editing a boundary** is different from **EDIT ROOM → SAVE LAYOUT**, which saves the objects arranged inside a room. When you're working in a Room, use its Blueprint.

**You're done when:** the new boundary appears on the correct space.
