# Find and organize equipment in a rack

A rack can hold several units. A unit can contain panels, breakers and other components.

![Example rack, panel and its positions](/help/diagrams/en/rack.svg)

## Find a piece of equipment

1. Open **EXPLORE** and choose the right room.
2. Select the **Rack**.
3. Open the **Device** you want to inspect.
4. Choose the component you're interested in.

## View a panel

A **Panel** contains positions. Some may be **AVAILABLE** (free) and others **OCCUPIED** (in use).

For example, a panel with **24 positions** may have just one occupied and 23 available. This simply describes what is currently registered.

## Add a component (if your account allows it)

1. Open the unit that will contain the new component.
2. Select **+ ADD EQUIPMENT** or **MANAGE POSITIONS**, depending on the screen.
3. Choose a template from **WAREHOUSE** or use **ONE-OFF**.
4. Fill in the details and save with **Install Equipment** or **Create and install**.
5. Reopen the unit and check that the part is in the correct position.

## If a position is unavailable

It may already be occupied or reserved. Check its status before trying to add a component.

**You're done when:** you can find equipment, recognize its parts and identify free and occupied positions.
