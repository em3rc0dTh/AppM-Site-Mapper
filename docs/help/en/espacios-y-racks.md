# Draw a room and place a rack

The plan of a room is called a **Blueprint**. It shows where racks are located and lets authorized users organize their positions.

![Example room plan with a rack](/help/diagrams/en/rack.svg)

## Before you begin

Open the **Room** you want to work on. Making changes requires an account with editing access.

## Place a rack on the plan

1. Open the Room's **Blueprint**.
2. Select **EDIT ROOM**.
3. To draw an area, choose **+ Draw Bay / Cluster**.
4. You can add a free place with **+ Place Empty Position**.
5. Select **+ Place Rack**.
6. Enter the requested rack information and place it on the plan.
7. Check that its location is correct.
8. Select **SAVE LAYOUT**.
9. Visit another page and return to confirm the rack is still there.

## Why do I need to save the plan?

While you're editing, changes may be a draft. **SAVE LAYOUT** confirms the new layout.

## If a button is missing

If **EDIT ROOM** doesn't appear, check that you've opened a Room rather than the entire building. Your account may also have viewing access only.

**You're done when:** the rack appears in the correct room and you can open it to see the equipment inside.
