# Understand plans, labels and status messages

Site Mapper uses short names and status labels to show information about rooms and equipment.

![Example room and rack](/help/diagrams/en/rack.svg)

## On the plan

- **Room:** the room you're viewing.
- **Bay:** an area inside the room.
- **Position:** a place where a rack can be located.
- **Rack:** the cabinet containing equipment.

Select an element to view its name and open its details.

## In a panel

- **AVAILABLE:** a free position.
- **OCCUPIED:** an occupied position.

## In power connections

- **CONNECTED:** a connection is recorded.
- **Feed A / Feed B:** named power routes.

## In equipment readings

- **LIVE:** a recent reading is available.
- **STALE:** the latest reading is old.
- **NO DATA:** no reading is available.

![Recorded information and readings are different](/help/diagrams/en/readings.svg)

## Remember

A symbol or label describes **what Site Mapper has recorded or received**. Follow your organization's normal procedure to confirm physical conditions.
