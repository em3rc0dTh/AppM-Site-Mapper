# Sign in and use your account

Learn how to enter Site Mapper and find the account options available to you.

## Sign in

1. Open the Site Mapper address given to you by your organization.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **SIGN IN**.
4. Your **Workspace** will open.

If you are asked to change your password during your first visit, enter your current password and a new one. Then sign in again using the new password.

## If you cannot sign in

Check your email and password. If the problem continues, ask the person who manages accounts at your organization. Never send your password in messages or screenshots.

## Why are some options missing?

The features you can see depend on your account type.

| Account        | What you can do                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Standard**   | View spaces, equipment, connections and readings |
| **Admin**      | Also organize spaces and equipment where allowed |
| **Superadmin** | Also manage user accounts                        |

## Add a user (Superadmin only)

1. Open **ADMIN → Settings → Users**.
2. Select **+ ADD USER**.
3. Enter the person's **Name**, **Email** and **Role**.
4. Set a temporary password and choose **Create user**.
5. Check that the account appears in the list.

Share the sign-in details securely. The new user should change the temporary password when they first sign in.

**You're done when:** you can open your Workspace and understand which options are available to you.
