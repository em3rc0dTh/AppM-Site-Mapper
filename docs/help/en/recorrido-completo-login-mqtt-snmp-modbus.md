# View circuits and power inputs

Some power distribution units let you inspect **individual circuits** and the **main power inputs** separately.

![Circuit readings and power input readings](/help/diagrams/en/readings.svg)

## View individual circuits

1. Open the **Device** you're interested in.
2. Find **PHYSICAL VIEW**.
3. Open the **Panel** that contains the circuits.
4. Browse the positions and choose the breaker you want.
5. Review its available voltage, current, power or energy readings.

## View the main inputs

1. Return to the **Device**.
2. Open **INSPECTOR → Inputs**, if available.
3. Look at inputs labelled **A**, **B** or other names.
4. To view previous readings, open **Charts** and choose a period such as **24H**.

## Why might the values differ?

A circuit and a main input are measured at different locations. They are not expected to always show the same reading.

## If some readings are missing

That input may not yet be set up or may not have sent recent values. You can still view circuits that do have readings.

**You're done when:** you can find individual circuit readings and main input readings without mixing them up.
