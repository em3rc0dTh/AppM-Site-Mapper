# Remove or archive an item

In Site Mapper, **removing something from a room plan** and **archiving a component** are different actions. Both deserve care so related information stays intact.

## 1. Remove an item from the room plan

![EDIT ROOM shows Remove selected inside an unsaved draft](/help/screenshots/edit-room.png)

1. Open the **Room** and its **Blueprint**.
2. Select **EDIT ROOM**.
3. Select the zone, position or rack you want to remove.
4. Use **Remove selected**, if available.
5. Review the plan before selecting **SAVE LAYOUT**.

**If removal is blocked:** a zone may still contain positions; a position may hold a rack; a rack may hold equipment or occupied space. Inspect what is inside first. Don't remove other objects simply to make the button available.

**Remember:** the change may still be a draft until you save the layout.

![EDIT CAS shows reservations and allocations that may block rack removal](/help/screenshots/rack-cas.png)

## 2. Archive an Equipment component

For accounts with editing access, some **Equipment** pages provide an **Archive** button.

1. Open the correct component.
2. Check whether it contains other parts or is assigned a location.
3. If **Archive** is enabled, confirm that it is the right item.
4. Check the updated status on its page.

The button may be unavailable when children, mounting or other conditions prevent removal.

## 3. Can I permanently delete a Site?

**Permanent Site deletion is not a regular Site Mapper action.** The system has archiving rules, but there is not yet a complete general-purpose Site archive/restore journey in every space page. Don't look for a “Delete Site” control or try to remove a location containing buildings and equipment.

## What about connections and readings?

Before removing equipment linked to terminals, connections or readings, ask the person responsible for that installation. Removing an item is not the same as erasing its history.

**You're done when:** you know which removal action belongs to each screen and when to stop.
